# 5.2 Notifications & events

Email is only for problems you must act on; everything else stays in the app.

| Event | Recipient | Channel | Timing | Content summary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No successful backup in 26 hours | Owner | Email and System page | Within 1 hour of the check; repeats daily until fixed | Job, error, last success |
| Disk usage above 85% | Owner | Email and System page | On crossing, then daily | Usage and largest folders |
| A unit is killed for memory, or restarts more than 3 times in 1 hour | Owner | Email | Within 15 minutes | Unit name and time; no user data |
| Sign-in locked (BR-29) | Owner | Email | Immediately | Time and tailnet device name |
| Token expires in 7 days | Owner | Email | 7 days before expiry | Token name and scope |
| Holidays missing for next year | Owner | Email once, and a Budget warning | 1 December | The missing year |
| New Unreviewed transactions | Owner | Badge in the app | Next page load | Count |
| Cycle opened | Owner | Banner in the app | Payday | New cycle dates and recurring items created |
| Phone changes waiting or refused | Owner | Sync status in the phone app, and the "Couldn't sync" list | At once | How many are waiting; each refused change with its reason (FR-41) |
| A phone change conflicts | Owner | The conflict screen in the phone app | On the sync that finds it | Both values of each field changed on both sides (FR-42) |

Internal events between modules: `fiscus.cycle.opened` and `fiscus.transaction.ingested`, published on the in-process event bus.
