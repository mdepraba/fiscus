# 4.3 State lifecycle

Five things change state: transactions, import batches, API tokens, pay cycles and the phone app's queued changes. Terminal states are marked and cannot be reversed.

**Transaction:** `Unreviewed`, `Confirmed`, `Trashed`, `Purged` (terminal)

&#91;embedded content: Transaction lifecycle · 4 states\]

| From | Trigger / event | Actor | Guard condition | To | Side effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (new) | Quick entry, private API create, import commit | Owner, local client | Valid per BR-01 to BR-09 | Confirmed | History entry; duplicate tag per BR-07 |
| (new) | Ingest call, recurring rule | Ingest client, scheduler | Valid; recurring: none yet this cycle (BR-33) | Unreviewed | Shows in Review; counts in budgets (A-08) |
| Unreviewed | Confirm, optionally after editing | Owner, local client (write) | — | Confirmed | History entry |
| Confirmed | Edit | Owner, local client (write) | Valid | Confirmed | Previous values to history |
| Unreviewed or Confirmed | Trash, or rollback of its import | Owner, local client (write) | Rollback within 7 days (BR-27) | Trashed | Leaves lists and budgets; previous state stored |
| Trashed | Restore | Owner, local client (write) | Within 30 days | Its previous state | History entry |
| Trashed | 30 days pass, or Empty trash | Scheduler, owner | — | Purged (terminal) | Record and history deleted; audit log keeps id, amount and date |

**Import batch:** `Uploaded`, `Previewed`, `Committed`, `Rolled back` (terminal), `Discarded` (terminal), `Failed` (terminal)

| From | Trigger / event | Actor | Guard condition | To | Side effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (new) | Upload TSV | Owner | Within BR-27 limits | Uploaded | File stored for 24 hours |
| Uploaded | Parse succeeds | System | — | Previewed | Preview with row errors and duplicates |
| Uploaded | Parse fails (not a TSV, wrong columns) | System | — | Failed (terminal) | Error names line and column; file deleted |
| Previewed | Commit | Owner | No unresolved row errors | Committed | Rows saved as Confirmed in one database transaction |
| Previewed | Discard, or 24 hours pass | Owner, scheduler | — | Discarded (terminal) | File deleted |
| Committed | Roll back | Owner | Within 7 days of commit | Rolled back (terminal) | Batch rows move to Trashed |

A Committed batch becomes final when its 7-day rollback window closes.

**API token:** `Active`, `Expired` (terminal), `Revoked` (terminal)

| From | Trigger / event | Actor | Guard condition | To | Side effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (new) | Create | Owner | BR-28 | Active | Value shown once |
| (new) | Pairing: the phone app sends the code the web app shows (FR-34) | Owner, phone app | Code unused and under 10 minutes old (BR-37) | Active | Named after the phone; the value goes only to the phone's secure store; audit entry |
| Active | Expiry date passes | System | — | Expired (terminal) | Calls get 401; warning email 7 days before |
| Active | Revoke | Owner | — | Revoked (terminal) | Calls get 401; audit entry; a paired phone stops syncing and asks to be paired again |

To renew, create a new token; expired and revoked tokens never come back.

**Pay cycle:** `Upcoming`, `Open`, `Closed` (terminal)

| From | Trigger / event | Actor | Guard condition | To | Side effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (new) | Payday rule and holidays give the next payday | System | Payday rule set | Upcoming | Dates shown on Budget |
| Upcoming | Holiday list or payday rule changes | Owner | — | Upcoming | Dates recomputed (EC-09) |
| Upcoming | Payday starts, 00:00 server time | Scheduler | — | Open | Previous cycle closes; recurring rules run |
| Open | Next payday starts | Scheduler | — | Closed (terminal) | Configuration snapshot stored (BR-23) |

A Closed cycle cannot reopen, but its transactions stay editable (BR-24).

**Queued change (phone app):** `Waiting`, `Conflict`, `Couldn't sync`, `Applied` (terminal), `Discarded` (terminal)

| From | Trigger / event | Actor | Guard condition | To | Side effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (new) | A change made on the phone | Owner | Valid per BR-01 to BR-08 on the phone (FR-37) | Waiting | Shows at once, marked "Waiting to sync" |
| Waiting | Push answers applied | Phone app | — | Applied (terminal) | The row takes the server's version and leaves the queue |
| Waiting | Push answers conflict | Phone app | — | Conflict | The phone pulls and merges field by field (FR-42) |
| Waiting | Push rejects an edit with `transaction_trashed` | Phone app | — | Conflict | Offers Restore and apply, or Discard (EC-24) |
| Conflict | No field changed on both sides, or the owner keeps one version of each | Phone app, owner | — | Waiting | Only the fields the phone changed are sent again |
| Conflict | Restore and apply, for a transaction trashed elsewhere | Owner | — | Waiting | A restore is queued before the edit (EC-24) |
| Waiting | Push rejects it for any other reason, or a change it depends on is rejected | Phone app | — | Couldn't sync | The reason shows, with Edit and Discard (FR-41) |
| Couldn't sync | Edit | Owner | Valid | Waiting | Sent again as a new mutation; the row keeps its id |
| Conflict or Couldn't sync | Discard | Owner | — | Discarded (terminal) | The phone's row goes back to the server's version, or is removed if the server never had it |

A change also waits while the phone is offline; nothing in the queue expires.
